# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Existing Next.js 16 App Router application using JavaScript and npm (inferred from the repository).

## Users

People in Vadodara seeking medical skin and hair care, with cosmetic treatments alongside, as confirmed by the user on 10 September 2026. Caregivers may also be evaluating care for a family member.

## Product Purpose

Shreemay Skin Clinic helps prospective patients understand the clinic's doctor-led dermatology care and take the next step through appointment booking, phone, WhatsApp, or directions. Success means a visitor can quickly understand the clinic, trust the care path, and contact the clinic.

## Positioning

Care is led by Dr. Hiteshree Shah, MBBS, MD Dermatology, with medical, cosmetic, laser, hair, vitiligo, and dermatosurgery services presented through one clinic.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive on a public marketing site and browse the home page or a treatment detail page. Primary actions leave the site for the external appointment calendar, phone, WhatsApp, Google Maps, or Instagram.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Home page with clinic overview, doctor profile, treatment categories, consultation flow, clinic imagery, patient reviews, education content, and visit details.
- Treatment detail routes for acne, hair fall, vitiligo, laser hair removal, PRP therapy, and chemical peeling.
- External booking, phone, WhatsApp, Google Maps, and Instagram links must remain functional.
- Do not fabricate medical outcomes, prices, availability, testimonials, certifications, or patient claims.
- Preserve the existing real clinic, doctor, logo, and treatment imagery unless a replacement is explicitly supplied.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Shreemay Skin Clinic, also referred to as Shreemay Clinic Vadodara.
- Doctor: Dr. Hiteshree Shah.
- Location: Akshar Chowk / Old Padra Road, Tandalja, Vadodara, Gujarat 390012.
- Existing logo, photography, and medical credentials are source assets and factual context.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real imagery in `public/images/`, including the doctor, clinic reception, consultation desk, treatment area, signage, and logo.
- Existing treatment data in `src/data/treatments.js`.
- Existing patient review copy and Instagram reel metadata in the application source.
- Existing booking, phone, WhatsApp, Google Maps, and Instagram URLs in the application source.

## Product Principles

- Make the doctor and care path clear before asking for conversion.
- Help visitors self-identify the concern they came to solve.
- Explain next steps without promising a one-size-fits-all outcome.
- Keep contact and booking available at every high-intent moment.
- Use real clinic evidence and plain language to earn trust.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Implement the public site to a practical WCAG 2.2 AA baseline: semantic structure, visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, meaningful image alternatives, reduced-motion support, and usable mobile touch targets.
